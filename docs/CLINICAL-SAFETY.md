# Clinical safety case and hazard log

Version 0.1 (draft) – prepared for the first controlled pilot. Status: **not yet approved by a
clinical safety officer.**

This document follows the structure of ISO 14971 (application of risk management to medical
devices) and the DCB0129 approach to clinical risk management for health IT manufacturers. It
records the hazards identified for Pacient, the controls built into the software and the residual
risk, and it states plainly what the software does **not** control and what the deploying
organisation must still do. Every software control cited below exists in this repository; the
file (and, where one exists, the automated test) that implements it is referenced so that a
reviewer can check it.

## 1. Scope and intended use

### 1.1 System

Pacient is an electronic health record and hospital information system: a patient portal and
workspaces for doctors, nurses, pharmacists, reception, facility directors and the Ministry of
Health, with modules for outpatient care, laboratory and imaging orders, emergency department,
inpatient wards, operating theatres, births, blood bank, nutrition, medication safety, early
warning (NEWS2), vaccination, notifiable disease surveillance, billing, video visits and partner
interfaces. Architecture and module boundaries are described in [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md);
security controls in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

### 1.2 Intended use

- Recording, retrieving and sharing patient information among authorised clinicians within the
  access rules of `apps/api/src/modules/access/policy.ts`.
- Supporting clinical workflows (orders, results follow-up, handover, scheduling, prescribing,
  dispensing, transfusion, theatre lists) with **decision support that informs, but never
  replaces, clinical judgement**.
- Giving patients and guardians access to their own released information.
- Aggregate, privacy-preserving reporting for facility management and the Ministry.

### 1.3 Intended users

Registered clinicians (doctors, nurses, pharmacists, radiology and blood bank staff), reception and
administrative staff, facility directors, Ministry officials, patients and verified guardians, and
contracted partner systems (laboratories, the Health Insurance Fund, civil registry, public
health) through scoped API keys.

### 1.4 Out of scope / not intended for

- Pacient is **not a certified medical device** and has not undergone conformity assessment. Any
  function that would qualify as a medical device (for example decision support that drives
  treatment) must be assessed before such use.
- **Diagnosis from images displayed by the built-in viewer.** The development image archive
  produces synthetic, watermarked images (`apps/api/src/modules/pacs/demo.ts`); even with a real
  PACS connected the browser viewer is a review tool, and the viewer states that diagnosis
  requires a certified diagnostic workstation (`apps/web/src/pages/imaging/ImageViewer.tsx`).
- **Clinical reliance on the bundled knowledge bases** until replaced or validated: the drug
  interaction set (`apps/api/src/modules/safety/knowledge.ts`, labelled "curated demo set"), the
  national tariff (`apps/api/src/modules/billing/tariff.ts`, demo values), the vaccination
  calendar (`apps/api/src/modules/vaccination/calendar.ts`, to verify with IKShPK) and the
  notifiable disease list (`apps/api/src/modules/surveillance/diseases.ts`, to verify with
  IKShPK).
- Use as the **primary** channel for time-critical alerts. In-app, email and SMS notifications
  supplement, but do not replace, direct clinical communication (telephone, bleep, verbal
  handover).
- Use with real patient data in development or test environments.

## 2. Risk method

Each hazard is rated for **likelihood** of harm occurring and **severity** of that harm, before
controls (initial risk) and after the controls listed (residual risk).

| Likelihood | Meaning                                            |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Very high  | Certain or almost certain; expected regularly      |
| High       | Not certain but very possible; reasonably expected |
| Medium     | Possible                                           |
| Low        | Could occur but uncommon                           |
| Very low   | Negligible or nearly negligible possibility        |

| Severity     | Meaning                                                                  |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Minor        | Minor injury or inconvenience, no lasting effect                         |
| Significant  | Minor injury requiring intervention, or significant psychological trauma |
| Considerable | Severe injury or incapacity from which recovery is expected              |
| Major        | Permanent incapacity or single death                                     |
| Catastrophic | Multiple deaths or permanent incapacity                                  |

Risk rating (1 = acceptable, 5 = unacceptable):

| Likelihood ↓ / Severity → | Minor | Significant | Considerable | Major | Catastrophic |
| ------------------------- | :---: | :---------: | :----------: | :---: | :----------: |
| Very high                 |   3   |      4      |      4       |   5   |      5       |
| High                      |   2   |      3      |      3       |   4   |      5       |
| Medium                    |   2   |      2      |      3       |   3   |      4       |
| Low                       |   1   |      2      |      2       |   3   |      4       |
| Very low                  |   1   |      1      |      2       |   2   |      3       |

Acceptability: **1 – acceptable**; **2 – acceptable, monitor**; **3 – tolerable only with
documented additional controls and CSO sign-off**; **4–5 – unacceptable, must be reduced before
go-live**. Residual ratings below assume the "remaining actions" column has been completed where
it says _before go-live_.

## 4. Residual risk summary and conditions for go-live

With the controls above, most hazards are reduced to ratings 1–2. **Twenty-one hazards remain at
rating 3** (H01, H03, H08, H11–H16, H19, H21, H23–H24, H26, H33, H35–H36, H39, H42–H43, H45);
they are tolerable only with the remaining actions in their rows and CSO sign-off. **No hazard is
accepted at rating 4 or 5**, which depends on the following conditions being met before any use
with real patients:

1. A clinical safety officer is appointed and approves this case.
2. **Medicines:** the demo interaction set and the demo dose limits are replaced by a licensed,
   maintained drug knowledge base (H13, H16).
3. **Reference content:** the official IKShPK vaccination calendar and notifiable disease list,
   and the national tariff, are loaded and validated (H35, H36).
4. **Translations:** clinical review of all Albanian and Serbian texts (H45).
5. **Imaging:** a certified PACS and diagnostic workstations are used for diagnosis; the
   accession-number workflow is validated with the modalities (H11).
6. **Laboratory interfaces:** each LIS interface is validated with test messages before go-live
   (H06).
7. **Security:** independent penetration test and security review; findings resolved (H37).
8. **Accessibility:** formal WCAG 2.2 AA evaluation, including screen readers (relevant to H01,
   H45).
9. **Business continuity:** the downtime procedure (`docs/DOWNTIME.md`) and a point-in-time
   restore are rehearsed; recovery objectives agreed (H42, H43).
10. **Training** of every user group on their workflows, positive patient identification,
    escalation and the limits of decision support.
11. **Incident reporting:** a route for users to report safety incidents and near misses, reviewed
    by the CSO, with feedback into this log.
12. **Local escalation protocols** for critical results, NEWS2, ED triage and transfusion
    reactions, with telephone/bleep as the primary channel; an approved paediatric (PEWS) chart
    (H08, H23, H26, H39).

## 5. Change management

- Every change that touches a clinical workflow, decision support, access rules, notifications,
  reference content or data migration is assessed against this log **before merge**: the author
  states in the pull request which hazards are affected, whether a new hazard arises and which
  test demonstrates the control.
- New hazards are added with the next free ID; existing rows are updated, never deleted (closed
  hazards are marked "closed" with the reason and date).
- Controls must be covered by automated tests in `apps/api/test` where technically possible; the
  CI pipeline (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`) runs them on every change.
- Safety-relevant reference content (interaction database, tariff, vaccination calendar,
  notifiable diseases) is changed only by the authorised role in the application, which is
  audited, and its validation source is recorded.
- The CSO reviews this log at least every release and after every reported incident; the version
  and approval date are recorded at the top of this document.
- Incidents and near misses reported by users are analysed for new causes and lead to updated
  ratings or new controls.
