# Albanian (sq) glossary

Standard Albanian as used in Kosovo's health system. Address patients formally (**ju**, imperative
in the polite plural: "Shtoni", "Hapni", "Zgjidhni"). Keep sentence case, as in the English UI.
Keep placeholders such as `{name}` and `{count}` exactly.
